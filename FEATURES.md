# Vega — Feature Guide

Vega is a simple, stress-free personal task manager designed to help you decide what to do next without getting overwhelmed by endless to-do lists.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Smart Fast Capture (Type Like You Speak)
- **Natural Language Typing**: Type tasks just like you would say them out loud—such as *"Buy groceries tomorrow at 5pm urgent"* or *"Call doctor next Monday"*.
- **Automatic Organization**: Vega automatically picks out dates, times, and priority levels, cleaning up the text so your task title stays neat and clutter-free.
- **Instant Shortcuts**: Add thoughts in seconds from the app, a home screen widget, or quick shortcuts without breaking your workflow.

---

### 2. Simple 4-Step Pipeline
Instead of complex folders and nested lists, Vega keeps your work moving smoothly through four simple stages:
- **Inbox**: A quick holding area for ideas and thoughts when you aren't sure yet when you'll do them.
- **Today**: Your main dashboard focusing strictly on what needs your attention right now.
- **Upcoming**: A clear view of all tasks planned for future days.
- **Done**: A history of your completed accomplishments.

---

### 3. Focus-First View (No Overwhelm)
- **Next Best Action**: Vega highlights the single most important task at the top of your screen based on deadlines and urgency, eliminating decision paralysis.
- **Clutter Control**: Keeps your list manageable by displaying only your top tasks on screen at one time, hiding the rest behind a simple expander so you never feel stressed by long lists.

---

### 4. Evening Snooze & Daily Reset Ritual
- **End-of-Day Check-in**: A guided evening prompt walks you through any unfinished items left in your Today list.
- **Fresh Starts**: Easily push tasks to tomorrow, keep them, complete them, or delete them so you can end every evening with a clean slate and peace of mind.

---

### 5. Flexible Recurring Tasks
- **Habit & Chore Automation**: Set routines to repeat automatically—daily, weekly, monthly, or on custom intervals—so recurring responsibilities stay on schedule effortlessly.

---

### 6. Gentle Reminders & Notifications
- **Timely Alarms**: Get notified right when time-sensitive tasks are due.
- **Daily & Weekly Reviews**: Optional prompts in the evening to wrap up your day and prepare for the week ahead.

---

### 7. Intuitive Swipe Gestures
- **Swipe Right**: Mark a task complete in one quick motion.
- **Swipe Left**: Postpone or delete tasks without needing extra taps.

---

### 8. Home Screen Widget
- **Instant Access**: Add new tasks directly from your phone's home screen without opening the main application.

---

### 9. Tags & Fast Search
- **Category Tags**: Group related tasks with simple, color-coded tags.
- **Instant Search**: Easily search across all your current, upcoming, and completed tasks.

---

### 10. Private & 100% Offline
- **On-Device Storage**: Everything stays 100% private on your phone.
- **No Internet Required**: No sign-ups, no tracking, no cloud servers, and full functionality even without an internet connection.

---

### 11. Dark & Light Themes
- **Easy on the Eyes**: Seamlessly switches between light and dark modes (or follows your device system preference) for comfortable day and night viewing.
